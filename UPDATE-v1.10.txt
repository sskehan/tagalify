  tagalify/app.js
  tagalify/core.js
  tagalify/style.css

  tagalify/README.md

Then close Tagalify completely and open it again (twice if needed). More > About should say 1.10.

Using the RODE Wireless PRO:
 1. Plug the receiver into the phone's USB-C port and switch the transmitter on.
 2. Open Tagalify > More > Open recording studio.
 3. In the new Microphone card tap "Test microphone" and allow the mic if asked.
 4. Choose the RODE (may show as "USB audio" / "USB headset") under "Record with".
 5. Speak into the transmitter - the bar should move. Tick "turn off phone noise filters" for a cleaner sound.
 6. Record as normal. The choice is remembered.
