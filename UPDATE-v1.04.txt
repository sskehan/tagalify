TAGALIFY v1.04 UPDATE PACK
==========================
This pack holds ONLY the files that changed since v1.03. Do not delete anything on GitHub.

What's new: Lessons 9 to 20 (book pages 52-100), in four new units on the Course page.

How to install
1. Unzip this pack. Inside the "tagalify" folder you will see the changed files in their folders.
2. On GitHub, open your Tagalify repository and upload each file into the SAME folder:
   - top level:        core.js, sw.js, app.js, README.md
   - data/:            course.json
   - data/lessons/:    l09.json to l20.json (new files)
   - tools/:           build.py and c11_l09.py to c22_l20.py (lesson sources, only needed for future updates)
   Use "Add file > Upload files" inside each folder, drag the files in, and Commit. Replace when asked.
3. Wait a minute, then open Tagalify on your phone. Close it fully and open it again once,
   and the new lessons appear. More > About shows v1.04.

Your progress, Dropbox backup and recordings are not affected.
