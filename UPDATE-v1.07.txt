TAGALIFY v1.07 CATCH-UP PACK  (from v1.03 straight to v1.07)
==============================================================
This pack holds every file that changed since v1.03, so you do NOT need the v1.04, v1.05 or v1.06 packs.
Do not delete anything on GitHub.

What's new since v1.03
- Lessons 9 to 34 (book pages 52-151), in eight new units on the Course page.
- Recording studio shows the English meaning under each Tagalog word and sentence.

How to install
1. Unzip this pack. Inside the "tagalify" folder are the changed files in their folders.
2. On GitHub, open your Tagalify repository and upload each file into the SAME folder:
   - top level:        core.js, sw.js, app.js, style.css, README.md
   - data/:            course.json
   - data/lessons/:    l09.json to l34.json (new files)
   - tools/:           build.py and c11_l09.py to c36_l34.py (lesson sources, only needed for future updates)
   Use "Add file > Upload files" inside each folder, drag the files in, and Commit. Replace when asked.
3. Wait a minute, then open Tagalify on your phone. Close it fully and open it again once.
   More > About should show v1.07.

Your progress, Dropbox backup and recordings are not affected.
